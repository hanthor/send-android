Send is a free and open-source messenger for Matrix, the open, decentralized chat network. It has a clean Material 3 interface built around your conversations, and it only opens end-to-end encrypted chats.

Send is a Matrix client: you need an account on a Matrix homeserver (for example matrix.org, or your own server). Send has no servers or accounts of its own.

Messaging
• End-to-end encrypted direct chats and groups, created encrypted by default
• Replies, edits, deletes, reactions with a full emoji picker, forwarding, multi-select and starred messages
• Photos, videos, files and voice messages, with an in-app photo and video viewer
• Typing indicators and read receipts ("Read" / "Seen by")
• Link previews fetched by your homeserver (can be turned off)
• Search inside a conversation

Conversations
• Pin, archive, mute, mark as unread and delete conversations
• Block people and manage blocked contacts
• Conversation details: members, shared media and files, notification settings

Calls
• Voice and video calls with Element Call (MatrixRTC), using the call server your homeserver provides
• Incoming calls ring full-screen, even over the lock screen
• Picture-in-picture during calls

Notifications without Google
• Push notifications through UnifiedPush with a distributor of your choice (for example ntfy)
• Pushes carry only event IDs, never message content
• Without a distributor, Send checks for new messages about every 15 minutes
• Reply and mark as read from the notification

Security and privacy
• Sign in with a password, single sign-on or a QR code from another session
• Type just your server name or Matrix ID; Send finds your homeserver
• Recovery key and key backup, emoji verification of your other sessions
• Manage your signed-in devices
• Local data encrypted with a key held in Android Keystore; no backups to the cloud
• No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads
